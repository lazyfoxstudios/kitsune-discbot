Kitsune OS is an independent project exploring conversational AI. This Discord experiment starts with a simple idea: a place for authorized users to chat with Kitsune.

AI can get things wrong. Treat replies as generated content, check important information and keep sensitive information out of the conversation. Features and availability may change during development.

Before you chat

Access is restricted. Read our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before using the private text-chat pilot. Each request is independent, and replies are visible in the authorized channel. This website does not provide access to the bot.
