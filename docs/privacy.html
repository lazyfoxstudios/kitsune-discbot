KITSUNE-OS-BOT · POLICY DRAFT

Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft · November 25, 2026

This draft is not a finalized notice of current bot processing. Data access, providers, storage, retention and deletion must be verified before use is enabled. No effective date has been set. Unresolved details below must be replaced with accurate disclosures before launch.

1. Operator, scope and contact

This draft concerns kitsune-os-bot, the private experimental Discord text-chat bot operated by Lazy Fox Studios, and this public information website. For privacy questions or a data request, contact LazyFoxStudios.VR@gmail.com. Discord, GitHub and email providers have their own privacy practices.

2. Proposed bot processing — verification pending

Providing text chat requires processing the submitted text and generating a reply. The pre-launch inventory must establish exactly which of the following the bot receives, uses or stores:

  • Message content, generated replies and conversation context.
  • Discord user, server, channel and message identifiers; usernames or display names; timestamps and command information.
  • Any surrounding message history, direct messages, attachments or other content accessible under the actual permissions and invocation method.
  • Operational records, errors, usage records and support correspondence.

This list identifies categories to check, not a claim that all are collected. The final policy must state actual collection and purposes, including any operation, debugging or abuse-handling uses. Access and visibility in Discord also depend on channel permissions and Discord's own behavior; a conversation with the bot should not be assumed confidential.

3. AI processing, recipients and other uses

The actual AI provider or providers, hosting services, processing locations, transfers and operator access have not yet been verified for this Discord bot. The final policy must identify the recipients or appropriate recipient categories, explain what each receives and why, and disclose any provider storage, human review or training use. This draft makes no local-only processing, no-training, no-sharing, no-sale or no-advertising assurance.

Any legally required disclosures or requests from authorities must also be addressed in the final policy. Do not send secrets, credentials, sensitive personal information or material you lack permission to share.

4. Storage, retention and security

Retention periods or clear determination criteria for messages, context, logs, identifiers, support correspondence and backups remain to be established and tested. This draft does not promise zero retention, a particular deletion period or automatic expiry. The final policy must explain what is retained, for how long and any applicable exceptions.

Access controls, safeguards and incident handling also require verification. No specific encryption, anonymity, confidentiality or security certification is claimed here. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Requests, choices and deletion

You can stop interacting with the bot. Server administrators can remove it or restrict its permissions. To ask about access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or other rights that may apply where you live, email LazyFoxStudios.VR@gmail.com. Provide enough context to locate the relevant interaction, such as a Discord user ID and approximate date; do not send passwords or tokens. Proportionate identity verification may be needed.

The actual request workflow, applicable response periods, deletion coverage, backup expiry and legal exceptions must be confirmed before launch. Removing a Discord message, stopping use or removing the bot should not be treated as proof that all related records have been deleted. Discord and other independent parties may retain their own copies under their policies. Applicable legal rights are not waived by this draft.

6. Website and contact email

This static website has no chat form, account system, analytics script, advertising tracker or third-party embedded content. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Images, styles and the animation pause script are served with the site. Opening external links takes you to the relevant provider; sending email shares your address and message with the studio mailbox and the email services involved.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical visitor information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Bot interactions on Discord are also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. These independent providers' policies are not replaced by this draft.

7. Eligibility and finalization

The bot is not intended for people below Discord's minimum age or the minimum age required by local law. The operator must confirm the intended audience, applicable jurisdictions, legal bases, minors' protections and any required consent or additional notices before enabling use.

Before launch, unresolved sections must be replaced with verified practices, the effective date set and a material-change notification method chosen. Version history will remain visible in the public repository. Contact the studio with questions while review is pending.
