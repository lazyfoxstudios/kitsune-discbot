KITSUNE-OS-BOT · PRIVATE CHAT & SPEECH PILOT

Privacy Policy

Private pilot policy · November 27, 2026

This policy applies to the restricted single-turn text-chat pilot and, when enabled, outbound synthesized speech (Phase 2A). Voice playback is an experimental feature subject to activation and testing. Publication is not a public invitation or a guarantee of availability. Please read this policy before using the bot.

1. Operator, scope and contact

This policy concerns kitsune-os-bot, the private experimental Discord chat and outbound speech bot operated by Lazy Fox Studios, and this public information website. For privacy questions or a data request, contact LazyFoxStudios.VR@gmail.com. Discord, GitHub and email providers have their own privacy practices.

2. Information processed and purposes

When you submit /chat in the authorized channel, the connector receives your message and Discord interaction metadata, including application, interaction, user, server and channel identifiers and any account information Discord includes in that interaction. It uses the identifiers to authorize the location, ignore bot users, prevent duplicate processing, apply request limits and deliver the reply.

Only your current message and fixed character instructions are sent to the local AI service. The connector does not add your Discord identifiers, earlier channel messages, saved conversation history or Project OS account data to the AI request. Each invocation is a separate exchange. Direct messages, other channels and threads are rejected. The pilot does not process attachments, web searches or ordinary channel-message events.

Replies appear in the channel and are visible to people with access to it. Authorized channel members and administrators may see or retain messages. Do not send credentials, sensitive personal information or confidential material. Do not share someone else's information without appropriate permission.

When outbound speech is enabled, the bot receives voice-state metadata supplied by Discord, including member and voice-channel identifiers, to check channel membership, authorize commands and manage playback and disconnection. Text submitted through /say and eligible generated chat replies may be sent to the operator-controlled local speech synthesis service. The bot is designed to join self-deafened, without participant audio capture, listening, recording or transcription. Generated audio is transmitted to Discord and may be heard or retained by other voice-channel participants. Do not submit sensitive or confidential text for playback.

3. AI processing, recipients and other uses

Discord carries commands, replies, voice-state events and synthesized audio. Speech synthesis uses the operator-controlled local speech service; the bot is not configured to send speech text to a cloud TTS provider. AI text generation runs on an operator-controlled local server using Ollama; the connector is not configured to send prompts to a cloud AI provider. The connector has no advertising, sale-of-data or model-training function. These statements describe this pilot's code and routing and do not replace independent providers' policies or promise that every surrounding system retains no information.

The operator can administer the service and its records, see channel content where authorized and read support correspondence. Email is handled by the services involved in delivering and storing it. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Discord, GitHub and email providers may process information internationally as described in their own notices.

4. Storage, retention and security

The connector does not save prompt/reply transcripts or conversation history to its own files or a Project OS database. It holds the current exchange and bounded admission records in process memory. User cooldown records expire after 15 seconds and are removed on the next admission check; up to 100 recent admission times cover a rolling hour; up to 512 admitted interaction IDs remain until displaced or the process stops. These limits are not secure memory-erasure guarantees.

The connector writes fixed operational events and health flags rather than prompt/reply text. Its logs rotate at approximately 1 MB per file with two files retained. The local inference service keeps operational logs with a configured size-based rotation of 10 MB per file and three files; debug logging is off. The reviewed proxy has access logging off. Size limits do not establish a fixed number of retention days, and unusual failures or surrounding systems may create additional diagnostic records.

Speech requests, generated audio, playback queues and voice-control state are handled temporarily in memory by the connector; it does not intentionally write synthesized audio or participant recordings to persistent storage. Stopping playback does not guarantee immediate termination of upstream synthesis or deletion of other participants' copies. These statements describe the connector, not a universal retention guarantee for surrounding services.

Discord holds channel messages separately. Support email and any administrative records are not subject to the connector's memory or log limits. Backup copies and independent providers' records are outside this connector's deletion mechanism; no fixed calendar retention or complete backup-deletion guarantee is made here. Contact the operator about a specific record or request.

The pilot uses a restricted channel, exact request-location checks, a separately stored bot credential and a limited service container. These controls do not guarantee confidentiality or absolute security. The local AI network path is not represented as end-to-end encrypted. No security certification is claimed.

5. Requests, choices and deletion

You can stop interacting with the bot. Server administrators can remove it or restrict its permissions. To ask about access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or other rights that may apply where you live, email LazyFoxStudios.VR@gmail.com. Provide enough context to locate the relevant interaction, such as a Discord user ID and approximate date; do not send passwords or tokens. Proportionate identity verification may be needed.

The operator will review requests for records under its control and explain relevant limits or legal exceptions. Applicable legal response requirements still apply; no additional fixed response-time promise is made. You or a server administrator can remove channel messages where Discord permits. Removing a message, stopping use or removing the bot does not prove deletion from Discord, other people's copies, logs or backups. Discord and other independent providers handle their records under their own policies. Applicable legal rights are not waived.

6. Website and contact email

This static website has no chat form, account system, analytics script, advertising tracker or third-party embedded content. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Images, styles and the animation pause script are served with the site. Opening external links takes you to the relevant provider; sending email shares your address and message with the studio mailbox and the email services involved.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical visitor information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Bot interactions on Discord are also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. These independent providers' policies are not replaced by this policy.

7. Eligibility and changes

The bot is for authorized pilot participants who meet Discord's minimum age and the minimum age required by local law. See the Terms of Service. Do not use it if you do not meet those requirements.

Material changes to the pilot's data handling will be described here and announced in the authorized channel before the changed processing begins where required. Version history is available in the public repository. Broader access or additional features require a further review and updated disclosures.
